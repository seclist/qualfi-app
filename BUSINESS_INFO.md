# Qualfi — Business Information & Model

## Overview
Normal static websites force customers to read text and fill out boring contact us forms and then wait for a reply. Qualfi micro-tools give users immediate, personalized value in exchange for contact details (aka high-intent leads).

* **Algorithmic Pre-Qualification**: Our tools pre-qualify leads via algorithms (0–100 score) and allow our clients to stop wasting time on low-quality leads and tire-kickers.
* **Frictionless Integration**: Our tools live either as a standalone fast-loading link via Instagram or TikTok bio, OR can be embedded directly into an already existing website or portal.
* **Instant Lead Notification (< 2s)**: Our tool will instantly notify our client of any high-quality leads via WhatsApp or SMS within 2 seconds of the form being completed, allowing them to hop onto the lead instantly while the buyer is actively engaged and eager to buy.
